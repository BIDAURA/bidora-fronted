BIDORA Privacy Policy

1. Overview

BIDORA is a lowest-unique-bid auction platform. You pay a small flat fee per bid, and the lowest unique bid at auction close wins the prize. We use your personal data to run accounts, auctions, payments, and support — and to prevent fraud and meet the law.

Key points:

  • We use your phone number with a one-time SMS code via SMS Ethiopia to protect your account.
  • We never see your mobile-money PIN. Payments go through Chapa and your chosen mobile-money provider.
  • Other bidders never see your phone number or bid amounts.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: phone number, display name, language preference, verification and sign-in records, device and IP information for security.
  • Bidding: bid amounts, prizes, times, bid status, collision events, auction results, and support messages.
  • Payments: payment method, payer phone number, fees, transaction references, and a transaction ledger. We never store your mobile-money PIN or full card details.
  • Notifications: push tokens, device details, SMS delivery records, and service messages.
  • Technical/security: IP address, device and app info, logs, and audit records. We do not collect precise location or use advertising trackers.
  • Permissions: we ask for notification permission where needed. You can change this in device settings.

3. How we collect data

  • Directly from you (registration, bidding, payment, support).
  • Automatically from your device and use of the app.
  • From service providers (payments, SMS, notifications, storage).

4. Why we use your data

  • Create, secure, and manage your account.
  • Verify your phone number.
  • Run auctions, detect collisions, and determine winners.
  • Process bid fees and winner payments, and handle refunds and disputes.
  • Send one-time codes and service notifications.
  • Provide support and resolve complaints.
  • Prevent fraud, enforce rules, and keep the platform safe.
  • Fix errors, improve reliability, and keep business and audit records.
  • Comply with the law and lawful requests.

5. Legal grounds

We process data because it is: necessary to perform our contract with you, based on your consent (including app permissions), required by law, or needed for legitimate interests such as security, fraud prevention, and service reliability — where your rights are not overridden.

You may withdraw consent at any time, but this does not affect processing already done lawfully. Some features cannot work without certain data.

6. What other users see

During an auction, others see only aggregate activity (e.g., number of bidders and bid events) — never your phone number or individual bids.

After an auction, the winner's display name and winning amount may be shown. Choose a display name you are comfortable sharing publicly. You can change it anytime.

7. When we share data

We share data only with service providers who host, store, send messages, process payments, or help secure the platform; professional advisers and auditors; authorities when required by law; or a successor in a business merger. We do not allow providers to use your data for advertising.

8. Service providers

  • Chapa and your mobile-money provider — payments.
  • SMS Ethiopia — SMS and one-time codes.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications.
  • Cloud storage — auction images.
  • Hosting, database, networking, and security providers — running BIDORA.

9. Storage and international processing

BIDORA is based in Ethiopia, but some providers operate infrastructure abroad. Your data may therefore be processed outside Ethiopia, where privacy rules may differ. We handle cross-border transfers in line with applicable requirements.

10. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed to run auctions, process payments, provide support, keep tax/accounting records, preserve auction integrity, and comply with law. When no longer needed, data is deleted or anonymised.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards (encryption, access controls, backups). You are responsible for protecting your phone, one-time codes, and account.

12. Your rights

You may request access to data, correct inaccurate data, object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to the competent Ethiopian data-protection authority.

13. Children

The Services are for people aged 18 or older. If we learn a child created an account, we will close it and delete the data, subject to law.

Terms of Service

Welcome to BIDORA. By using our platform, you agree to abide by our fair play bidding rules, payment processing terms, and auction integrity policies.

1. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and possess a valid Ethiopian mobile phone number to participate in BIDORA auctions.

2. Bidding Mechanics

Bids are non-refundable once placed. The participant with the lowest unique numerical bid when the auction countdown expires wins the specified prize.

3. Payments

All bid transactions are processed securely via Telebirr or CBE Birr through Chapa. Bids are committed after payment confirmation.
